# OMX Plan — Story 167.9: Enforce Account-Scoped Conditional Cabinet Settlement

## Outcome

Make shared cabinet creation transport and settlement conditional on the immutable initiating account/session/operation. A late result for account A cannot mutate live account B or drive B-visible toast, navigation, reset, marker clearing, or error UI. GREEN must consume Story 167.8's real merged contract; a mock-only endpoint is insufficient.

This is a non-route shared frontend prerequisite. It owns only the minimal `CabinetCreationForm` consumer seam required to handle a typed settlement result of `applied | stale | indeterminate`; Story 167.5 retains route, presentation, validation, recovery-marker implementation, and final E2E/freeze ownership. Story 167.9 adds no route-ledger row.

- **Owned/allowed:** `src/services/cabinets.service.ts`; inventoried `src/lib/api.ts` and `src/lib/api-client.ts` immutable request-context plumbing; auth store/session conditional-settlement helpers; direct login/session/guard coordination required by the shared boundary; the smallest reviewed `CabinetCreationForm` consumer hunk and direct test needed to branch on typed `applied | stale | indeterminate`; direct unit/integration/privacy tests and Story evidence.
