## What Was Challenging

### 1. Close-at-3 attempt was again structurally premature (2nd consecutive)

Story 115.1's 3rd-pass close decision optimized for Trigger 3 (1 ≤ 5) + Trigger 4 satisfied (blanket-qualified scope) — rule semantics satisfied. **But the user-invoked post-close 4th-pass mechanism (now codified by THIS story) operates independently of rule semantics**, and reverted the close-at-3 within minutes. This is exactly the Story 114.1-FE outcome reproduced.

**Lesson**: codifying a mechanism does not make stories closing at 3 safer; if anything, the codification makes the mechanism more likely to be invoked. Future codification stories should default to 4-pass scheduling rather than attempting 3-pass close.

### 2. Tasks 1-4 narrative shipped without pre-written block-level blanket qualifier

AC-8 + Dev Notes L100 explicitly prescribed the up-front blanket qualifier convention per Stories 113.2/114.1 precedent. Parent-session executor did NOT apply it — Tasks 1-4 narrative had 8+ unqualified meta-claims that 1st-pass F-1 caught. **Discipline gap**: future codification stories should pre-write the blanket qualifier at Tasks-1-4 commit time, not deferred to Post-1st-pass.

### 3. Char-count attestation self-induced drift recurred (3rd-pass F-1)

Post-2nd-pass tracking surface cited "(114 chars verified)" for Lesson 2 candidate. 3rd-pass re-measurement returned 109 chars (string was further trimmed since the original measurement). Identical self-inducement pattern to Story 113.2-FE 4th-pass F-1 + Story 115.1's own predicted F-6.

**Lesson**: char-count attestations decay quickly during multi-pass editing. Either re-measure at every pass OR drop inline char-count attestations and rely on the check-lessons-length validator.

### 4. The 4-pass empirical bound's "natural fixed point" framing is statistically weak (N=6)

CLAUDE.md Observation 1 documents the 4-pass bound across 6 stories converging on 4-pass chain length. But:
- 4 of the 6 are extended-from-3 by user-invoked 4th-pass (Stories 112.4 + 113.1 + 113.2 + 114.1 + 115.1; 5 of 6 actually — 112.4 had in-chain 4th-pass via Trigger 3)
- The "fixed point" narrative may be a self-fulfilling prophecy if coordinators invoke `/code-review` until chain reaches 4
- Story 112.3 is the only purely-in-chain 4-pass story (Triggers 1 + 3 escalation)

**Lesson**: future framing should distinguish "in-chain 4-pass bound" (single instance: 112.3) from "post-close-extended 4-pass bound" (4-5 instances). The current observation conflates them.

## Key Insights

### Insight 1: Codification's own evidence base grows by being read (deepest meta-recursion observed)

Story 115.1 codified user-invoked post-close 4th-pass as discipline metric, citing 4-of-4 record. User invoked exactly that mechanism on Story 115.1. The mechanism extended the cited record to 5-of-5. The codification's evidence base grew by virtue of being read + applied.

**Practical implication**: discipline-codification stories should expect their evidence-base attestations to be self-falsifying upon close (post-close 4th-pass extends the cited N). Pre-emptively hedge attestations with explicit conditional qualifiers ("as of close; may extend by 1 if user-invoked post-close 4th-pass runs").

### Insight 2: Close-at-3 is operationally provisional regardless of rule semantics

Trigger 3 (≤5 findings) + Trigger 4 (blanket-qualified) can both be satisfied at 3rd-pass, allowing close-at-3 per rule. But the user-invoked post-close 4th-pass mechanism is empirically 5-of-5 in catching attestation drift in close-row narrative. **Until the mechanism is no longer invoked, close-at-3 is provisional for discipline-codification stories.**

Operational recommendation: schedule 4-pass review for discipline-codification stories by default, treating user-invoked post-close as scheduled rather than optional.

### Insight 3: Block-level blanket qualifier convention is mature and reduces noise (declining finding density)

Epic 113 (Story 113.1): 19 findings without convention; Story 113.2 introduced convention mid-story (8 findings).
Epic 114 (Story 114.1): 16 findings with convention at Post-1st-pass (adopted reactively).
Epic 115 (Story 115.1): 14 findings with convention at Post-1st-pass (still adopted reactively — Tasks 1-4 narrative shipped without).

**The convention works. Adoption discipline (up-front vs reactive) is the remaining variable.** Pre-writing the qualifier at Tasks 1-4 commit time would likely drop finding density further.

### Insight 4: The 4-pass empirical bound has 2 distinct mechanisms producing it

CLAUDE.md Observation 1 documents the bound but conflates two mechanisms:
- **In-chain 4-pass** (Story 112.3 only): Triggers 1 + 3 escalation drives chain to 4
- **Post-close-extended 4-pass** (Stories 112.4 + 113.1 + 113.2 + 114.1 + 115.1): close at 3-pass, user-invoked post-close 4th-pass extends

The bound is robust to rule refinement (Insight 2 from Epic 114-FE retro) but the empirical mechanism is dual. Future Epic 116-FE codification might cleanly separate these two pathways in Observation 1.

### Insight 5: The recursive meta-narrative pattern is reproducible and predictable

Across Stories 113.1, 113.2, 114.1, 115.1, the recursive self-validation pattern reproduced with increasing precision:
- Story 113.1: discovered the meta-meta-pattern (4th-pass found "ALL THREE triggers" meta-claim added IN 3rd-pass itself)
- Story 113.2: codified Trigger 4 RECOMMENDED; story self-fired Trigger 4 in passes 1+2+3
- Story 114.1: promoted Trigger 4 MANDATORY; close-at-3 reverted by user-invoked 4th-pass; reversal confirms 4-pass bound
- Story 115.1: codified user-invoked post-close 4th-pass as discipline metric; close-at-3 reverted by user-invoked 4th-pass; reversal extends evidence base from 4-of-4 to 5-of-5

**Pattern is stable and predictable.** Future codification stories can be scheduled with explicit knowledge of: (1) 1st-pass will fire Trigger 4; (2) blanket qualifier suppresses 2nd+ passes; (3) 3rd-pass usually terminates Trigger 3 with ≤5; (4) user-invoked post-close 4th-pass extends chain to 4 + catches attestation drift.
