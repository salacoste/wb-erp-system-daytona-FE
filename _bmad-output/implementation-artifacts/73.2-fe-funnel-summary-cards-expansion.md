## Dev Notes

### Architecture: In-Place Expansion (Simple)

This is a straightforward expansion — no extraction needed. FunnelSummaryCards.tsx is 94 lines today. Adding 4 more card definitions (~20 lines) + 1 import line + 1 formatter = ~120 lines total. Well under the 200-line limit.

```
BEFORE (94 lines, 4 cards):
  FunnelSummaryCards.tsx — 4 metric cards

AFTER (~120 lines, 8 cards):

```

### Card Order (Logical Funnel Flow)

| # | Label | Field | Icon | Color | Format |
|---|-------|-------|------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Просмотры | `openCardCount` | Eye | text-blue-600 | formatNumber |
| 2 | Корзина | `addToCartCount` | ShoppingBag | text-amber-600 | formatNumber |
| 3 | Заказы | `ordersCount` | ShoppingCart | text-orange-600 | formatNumber |
| 4 | Выкупы | `buyoutCount` | PackageCheck | text-green-600 | formatNumber |
| **5** | **Сумма выкупов** | **`buyoutSumRub`** | **Banknote** | **text-emerald-600** | **formatRub** |
| **6** | **Конв. корзины** | **`cartConversion`** | **ArrowRightLeft** | **text-teal-600** | **formatPercent** |
| 7 | Сквозная конверсия | `totalConversion` | TrendingUp | text-indigo-600 | formatPercent |
| **8** | **Отмены** | **`cancelCount`** | **XCircle** | **text-red-600** | **formatNumber** |

### Semantic Color Rationale

- **Blue** (Просмотры): Neutral informational metric
- **Amber** (Корзина): Warm "interest" stage between view and order
- **Orange** (Заказы): Existing — action/order stage
- **Green** (Выкупы): Existing — positive outcome
- **Emerald** (Сумма выкупов): Money-related, positive financial metric
- **Teal** (Конв. корзины): Conversion metric — greenish indicates good funnel flow
- **Indigo** (Сквозная конверсия): Existing — overall funnel health
- **Red** (Отмены): Negative metric — cancellations
