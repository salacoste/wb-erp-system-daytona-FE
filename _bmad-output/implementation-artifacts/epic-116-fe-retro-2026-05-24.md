## Key Insights

### I-1 — Mechanism A and Mechanism B are NOT mutually exclusive
Pre-Story-116.1 framing implicitly suggested a story converges to 4-pass via one mechanism or the other. Story 116.1 demonstrated empirically that a single story can be subject to BOTH: Mechanism A (in-chain 4-pass with Trigger 3 firing throughout chain BEFORE Status flip) PLUS Mechanism B (user-invoked post-close 4th-pass AFTER Status flip). The mechanisms operate at different timeline phases of the same story.

### I-2 — User-invoked post-close 4th-pass is independent of in-chain mechanism that achieved 4-pass length
Story 116.1's 4-pass length came from Mechanism A (in-chain Trigger 3 firing 3 consecutive times). But the user-invoked post-close 4th-pass STILL surfaced 7 findings. The two mechanisms catch DIFFERENT defect classes — in-chain catches inter-pass propagation defects; user-invoked-post-close catches close-row attestation drift.

### I-3 — Close-row attestation drift is inevitable for high-N attestations
Empirically: close-row's own `**Lessons:**` line auto-ticks the lesson-line count by +1 — making any close-row "N lesson lines" attestation self-falsifying upon Status flip. Same defect class observed at Stories 113.2 (47/48), 114.1 ("3-of-3" → "4-of-4" Lesson 1 retraction), 115.1 ("4-of-4" → "5-of-5" record retraction), 116.1 ("50 lesson lines" → 51). The defect class is structural — not authorship failure.

### I-4 — Recursive self-validation has compounded for codification stories
Per Observation 4 (L285), recursive self-validation instances = 14+ per-codification-story basis OR ≥22+ per-recurrence-finding basis. The two metrics diverge because they count different things. Future codification stories must specify counting basis when citing the evidence.

### I-5 — Within-line YAML status entry propagation is a novel propagation-drift pattern
Cross-document grep + read-full-line is needed for within-line repetition detection. Standard grep-line-by-line propagation discipline (Pattern 4 § Fix-block propagation) does not catch within-line repetition in long YAML entries.

### I-6 — High-novel-narrative-volume stories produce denser user-invoked post-close 4th-passes
Story 116.1 (3 codifications) = 7 user-invoked findings; prior stories (1 codification each) = 1-3 findings each. Hypothesis worth tracking: should future codification epics split high-volume codification work across multiple single-codification stories?

## Lessons (Epic-level — distinct from story-level Lessons in Change Log)

1. **Pattern 4 is empirically permanent** across 14+ recurrences spanning 7 epics (97/111/112/113/114/115/116) — never trust author self-discipline alone for attestation-class invariants; always run multi-pass adversarial review with FRESH context.
2. **Mechanism A + Mechanism B are independent** — a story can be subject to BOTH; in-chain 4-pass termination via Trigger 3 silent does NOT preclude user-invoked post-close 4th-pass surfacing additional findings.
3. **Close-row attestation drift is structural** — any close-row "N lesson lines" or "N-of-N record" attestation auto-falsifies upon Status flip; pre-cover via dual-attestation or accept disclosure-via-APPEND.
4. **Within-line YAML propagation is a novel propagation-drift sub-pattern** — grep-line-by-line discipline must be augmented with read-full-line discipline for long status entries.
5. **High-novel-narrative-volume stories produce denser user-invoked post-close 4th-passes** — at N=6 sample size, Story 116.1 (3 codifications, 7 findings) breaks the prior 1-codification-per-story range (1-3 findings) at high end; track 1-2 more before committing to scope-split convention.
