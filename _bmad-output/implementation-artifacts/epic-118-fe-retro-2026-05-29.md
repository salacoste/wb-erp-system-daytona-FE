## Key Insights

### I-1 — 2 Mechanism B invocations on one story is a structural possibility, not an aberration
When a 1st Mechanism B pass surfaces fixes that themselves introduce defects (D4→D7), a 2nd Mechanism B pass becomes warranted. Implies: Mechanism B is potentially recursive, not single-shot. Empirically observed N=1 (Story 118.1). Should track if it recurs.

### I-2 — In-chain 4-pass discipline + Mechanism B are complementary, not redundant
Story 118.1's in-chain 4-pass converged at 0 findings (Trigger 3 silent), yet Mechanism B surfaced 7 additional findings (6+1). The two mechanisms catch DIFFERENT defect classes at DIFFERENT lifecycle moments:
- **In-chain**: inter-pass propagation defects (caught BEFORE Status flip)
- **Mechanism B**: close-row + sprint-status attestation drift (caught AFTER Status flip)

Confirms Story 116.1-FE Insight I-2 (Mechanism B independent of in-chain mechanism). **Counter-intuitive**: a perfect-quality in-chain 4th-pass does NOT satisfy or substitute for Mechanism B.

### I-3 — Proactive blanket qualifier scope is the unchanged leak surface across 2 stories
Both Story 116.1-FE 1st-pass F-6 and Story 118.1-FE 1st-pass F-5 caught the same leak: an absolute meta-claim in live canonical CLAUDE.md prose, NOT covered by the story-file-scoped blanket qualifier. **Structural property**: live-canonical-insertion meta-claims are outside the convention's scope. **Implication**: A-2 documentation should explicitly note this scope boundary; reviewers should know to scan live-CLAUDE.md-prose insertions for unqualified meta-claims separately.

### I-4 — Bundled multi-codification stories cluster Mechanism B findings around 6-7
Story 116.1-FE: 7 user-invoked findings on 1st Mechanism B pass; Story 118.1-FE: 6 + 1 = 7 across 2 Mechanism B passes. **Cluster appears stable at N=6-7 for 3-codification stories.** Suggests the bundle convention's overhead has a ceiling, not unbounded growth.

### I-5 — The convergence trajectory itself (5→2→1→0) is an underused observability metric
Story 118.1's convergence trajectory was cleanly monotonic, indicating mature codification subject matter. Earlier codification stories (Story 112.3-FE: 10+6+3+6, Story 112.4-FE: 10+8+1+6) showed non-monotonic late-pass spikes. **Hypothesis**: monotonic-decreasing convergence correlates with mature codification subject; non-monotonic correlates with novel-pattern stories. Future codification retros should report convergence trajectory as a standard metric.
